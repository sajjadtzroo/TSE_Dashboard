AI Engine

Ask anything about
any financial report.

The RAG chatbot ingests Codal PDF reports, indexes them with pgvector, and answers financial questions with live database access and cited sources.

  • ▸
    Automatic Codal PDF download, OCR extraction, chunking and embedding
  • ▸
    Multi-turn conversation with tool calling — live stock prices, order book, indices
  • ▸
    5 model choices: Gemini 2.0, GPT-4o, GPT-4o mini, Claude Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Pro
  • ▸
    Upload your own PDFs — processed in background, searchable instantly
